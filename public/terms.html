1. What QuoteScout Is

QuoteScout is an AI-powered risk-surfacing tool for precision manufacturing Request for Quote (RFQ) documents. Users upload RFQ documents and receive a structured risk report identifying potential compliance, sequence, dimensional, and outside-service risks.

2. What QuoteScout Is NOT

QuoteScout does not, and will never:

  • Price jobs or generate cost estimates
  • Guarantee manufacturability of any feature, part, or assembly
  • Replace engineering judgment
  • Recommend whether to bid or not bid on any job
  • Approve, reject, or certify any work

QuoteScout is decision-support software. All flags, summaries, and outputs require verification by qualified engineering personnel before any pricing, bidding, or production decisions are made.
