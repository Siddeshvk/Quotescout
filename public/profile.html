QuoteScout Analyze RFQ Shop Profile

Shop Capability Profile

QuoteScout uses this to score every RFQ against your actual capabilities, capacity, and red lines — not a generic shop. The more you fill in, the sharper the analysis. Stored privately in your browser only. Nothing leaves this device unless you click "Save Profile."

Why fill this out?
  • Flags requirements you can't meet (certs, materials, processes) before you waste time quoting
  • Catches unrealistic delivery dates against your real backlog
  • Dollarizes risk using your shop rate, not industry average
  • Distinguishes "we can do this" from "we can do this in spec, on time, profitably"
List spindle counts, makes, models, work envelopes. Be specific about what you DON'T have — that's a flag generator.
Click each cert you currently hold (not in-progress). Anything missing flags as a capability gap if the RFQ requires it.

Why fill this out?

  • Capability gaps surface early. RFQ wants NADCAP Welding and you don't have it? Flagged before you spend an hour quoting.
  • Material red lines respected. Listed material exclusion shows up in the RFQ → automatic NO-BID recommendation.
  • Realistic lead times. 3-week delivery on an 8-week-lead-time job? Flagged as a blocking clarification.
  • Dollarized risk uses YOUR rate. CMM time on a tight feature gets costed at your $/hr, not a generic estimate.
  • Outside process risk. RFQ requires gold plating but it's not on your AVL? Lead time + vendor risk flagged.
  • Private by design. Stored in your browser. Export to a file to back up or move to another machine.
