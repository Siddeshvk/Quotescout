How this changes the analysis

Without a profile, QuoteScout flags everything generically — "verify capability for NADCAP." With your profile loaded, every flag is framed against what your shop actually has:

  • Certs the customer requires that you don't currently hold become explicit capability gaps, not generic warnings
  • Features that exceed your machine envelope or CMM travel get flagged with the exact dimension mismatch
  • Outside services get checked against your AVL — covered or gap
  • Cost estimates use your stated shop rate, not generic industry numbers
  • Verdict is calibrated to your shop — "recommend no-bid" when there's a capability gap you don't currently have a path around
