How this is used

When you upload an RFQ, your profile is included with the analysis prompt. The AI will:

  • Flag certifications/processes the customer requires that your shop doesn't currently have
  • Recognize when a tolerance/feature is within your equipment envelope vs. requires an outside source or new tooling
  • Estimate inspection burden against your CMM travel envelope
  • Calibrate cost estimates against your shop rate
  • Mark "capability gap" flags vs. generic "verify capability" flags
