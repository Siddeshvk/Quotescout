Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 — Effective May 2026

QuoteScout takes your privacy and confidentiality seriously. This policy explains exactly what we collect, what we don't, and how it's handled.

1. What We Collect

1a. Information you provide directly

  • Your full name
  • Your work email address
  • Your company name
  • Your job title (optional)
  • Your acceptance timestamps for the NDA, Terms, and Privacy Policy

1b. Information generated by your use

  • Filenames of documents you upload (NOT the file content)
  • File size and page count
  • Timestamp of analysis
  • Flag count by severity (red/amber/yellow/purple)
  • AI model used and processing time
  • IP address and browser user-agent (for audit trail)

1c. What we do NOT collect or store

  • The content of any document you upload
  • The text extracted from your documents
  • The full AI risk-analysis output (we store only the flag-count summary)
  • Payment information (we do not charge during Free Beta)

2. How Files Are Handled

When you upload a document:

  1. The file is received over HTTPS/TLS-encrypted connection.
  2. The file is loaded into volatile memory on a serverless function.
  3. The text is extracted in memory.
  4. The text is checked for ITAR/EAR markers — if any are found, processing stops and the file is rejected.
  5. The extracted text is sent to Anthropic, PBC's Claude API for AI inference.
  6. The AI's structured response is parsed and returned to you.
  7. The file buffer and extracted text are cleared from memory.
  8. The temporary file on disk (if any) is deleted.
  9. A timestamp confirming deletion is included in your response.

The total time from upload to deletion is typically under 60 seconds. There is no persistent storage of file content anywhere in our infrastructure.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third parties to operate QuoteScout. Each is bound by their own privacy policy and contractual obligations to us:

  • Anthropic, PBC — provides the Claude AI model that performs the risk analysis. Anthropic's API data handling policy applies. Document text is sent to Anthropic for inference; per Anthropic's published API policy, API inputs are not used for model training.
  • Vercel, Inc. — hosts our website and runs our serverless functions. Standard web request logs may be retained per their policy.
  • Supabase, Inc. — hosts our database where metadata is stored.

4. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information or document metadata to any third party. We do not use your documents or feedback to train external models.

5. Use of Information

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • Provide the QuoteScout service to you
  • Maintain a legal record of your NDA, Terms, and Privacy acceptance
  • Communicate with you about the service (e.g., feedback requests, outage notices, product updates)
  • Improve our prompts and risk taxonomy (using metadata only — never your document content)
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. Data Retention

NDA acceptances and analysis metadata are retained for as long as you are a user, plus three (3) years after termination, to satisfy contractual and legal obligations. You may request earlier deletion by emailing us; we will honor such requests within 30 days unless prohibited by law.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address listed on the home page.

8. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect data: HTTPS/TLS in transit, encrypted databases at rest (provided by Supabase), restricted access controls, and the in-memory file processing described above. However, no system is perfectly secure. You acknowledge that you upload documents at your own risk.

If you are subject to GDPR or another regional data-protection regime, please contact us before using the service to confirm we can meet your requirements.
