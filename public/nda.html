This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement protects both your information and QuoteScout's trade secrets (prompts, taxonomy, and product feedback). The full terms are below, verbatim.

01

Parties & Effective Date

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between QuoteScout (operated by Sid K, doing business as an individual) and you, the individual or entity identified in the signature block, effective on the date of electronic signature ("Effective Date").

02

Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party to the other, including but not limited to: engineering drawings; RFQ packages; specifications; bills of materials; pricing data; supplier lists; manufacturing processes; quality clauses; software source code; analysis output; and any other information marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential under the circumstances.

03

Obligations

Each party agrees to: (a) hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely to evaluate, perform, or use the QuoteScout analysis service; (c) protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care; (d) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent, except to employees, contractors, or service providers who have a need to know and are bound by substantially similar confidentiality obligations.

04

Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known without breach of this Agreement; (b) was rightfully known by the receiving party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without reference to the Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations.

05

Data Handling Commitments

QuoteScout further commits that uploaded files are:

  • (i)processed in memory and deleted within sixty (60) seconds of analysis completion;
  • (ii)never stored beyond metadata (filename, size, timestamp, flag count) and transient processing data, which is cleared on completion;
  • (iii)transmitted only over HTTPS/TLS;
  • (iv)not used to train QuoteScout's or any third party's AI models;
  • (v)sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis, under Anthropic's data policy stating they do not train on API traffic;
  • (vi)for CAD files, additionally processed for geometry (dimensions, feature measurements — not the drawing itself) by QuoteScout's own geometry microservice hosted on Google Cloud Run, in memory, and not stored.
06

ITAR / Export-Control Restriction

This service is for non-ITAR, non-EAR-controlled commercial work only. You represent that any documents uploaded do not contain information subject to ITAR (22 CFR §§ 120–130), EAR (15 CFR §§ 730–774), Department of Defense classification, or other U.S. export-control regulations. QuoteScout's pre-screen will reject documents containing export-control markers; you remain solely responsible for export-control compliance.

07

Term & Termination

This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the Effective Date with respect to each item of Confidential Information disclosed during that period. Obligations survive termination as to information disclosed prior.

08

Disclaimers

The QuoteScout analysis service is provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind. QuoteScout does not approve, price, or guarantee the manufacturability of any RFQ. All flags, recommendations, and verifications produced by the service must be independently reviewed by qualified engineering personnel before any pricing or production decision is made.

09

Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, United States, without regard to its conflicts-of-laws principles.

10

Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties with respect to Confidential Information and supersedes all prior agreements. Any modifications must be in writing.
