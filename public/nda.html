Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Summary)

Effective Date: Date of acceptance below.
Parties: You (the individual or company submitting documents) and QuoteScout (operating as a sole proprietorship by the founder during Free Beta).

1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with the use of QuoteScout, including but not limited to: RFQ documents, drawings, part specifications, customer identities, pricing, manufacturing processes, shop capabilities, business strategies, product feedback, prompts, source code, prototypes, and any data labeled or reasonably understood to be confidential.

2. Mutual Obligations

3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the receiving party, (b) was known to the receiving party before disclosure, (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's information, or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided the receiving party gives prompt notice to allow the disclosing party to seek a protective order.

QuoteScout commits that: (a) uploaded files are processed in volatile memory only, (b) files are deleted within 60 seconds of analysis completion, (c) only metadata (filename, file size, page count, flag count, timestamp) is logged to QuoteScout's database, (d) file content is not retained, sold, shared, or used to train any model.
