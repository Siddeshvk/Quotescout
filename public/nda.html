2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party to the other, including but not limited to: engineering drawings; RFQ packages; specifications; bills of materials; pricing data; supplier lists; manufacturing processes; quality clauses; software source code; analysis output; and any other information marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential under the circumstances.

3. Obligations

Each party agrees to: (a) hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information solely to evaluate, perform, or use the QuoteScout analysis service; (c) protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care; (d) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent, except to employees, contractors, or service providers who have a need to know and are bound by substantially similar confidentiality obligations.

4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known without breach of this Agreement; (b) was rightfully known by the receiving party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without reference to the Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligations.

QuoteScout further commits that uploaded files are: (i) processed in memory and deleted within sixty (60) seconds of analysis completion; (ii) never stored beyond metadata (filename, size, timestamp, flag count); (iii) transmitted only over HTTPS/TLS; (iv) not used to train QuoteScout's or any third party's AI models; (v) sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis only, under Anthropic's data policy stating they do not train on API traffic.
