Sample flags

RED · COMPLIANCE · CONFIDENCE 95%
NADCAP heat-treat certification required for AS9100 deliverable

Drawing references AMS 2770 (heat treatment of wrought aluminum). Combined with the AS9100 quality clause referenced on page 4, the heat-treat processor must hold a current NADCAP HT certification. Verify shop-approved vendor list before committing.

Recommended: Confirm NADCAP-certified HT vendor available before quoting lead time.

Location: page 1 title block + page 4, quality clause QC-7

RED · SEQUENCE · CONFIDENCE 90%
Hard-coat anodize specified after final machining — masking required for ±0.0005" bores

Two precision bores (Ø 0.5000 +0.0005/-0.0000) are dimensioned post-anodize per drawing note 7. Hard Coat Type III adds 0.0005–0.001" per side. Bores must either be masked during anodize OR machined oversized pre-anodize. The print does not specify which.

Recommended: Clarify with customer: mask the bores, or quote with pre-anodize oversized machining + post-anodize honing?

Location: page 2, note 7 + bores at view A-A

AMBER · COMPLIANCE · CONFIDENCE 88%
First Article Inspection per AS9102 required before production

PO references AS9102 FAI. First piece must be measured against every drawing dimension, documented in AS9102 Form 1/2/3, and customer-approved before any further production. Adds 5–10 business days before production can ramp.

Recommended: Add FAI lead time buffer to delivery commitment. Confirm shop has CMM operator certified for AS9102 documentation.

Location: PO line 4, references AS9102 Rev C

YELLOW · OUTSIDE SERVICE · CONFIDENCE 82%
Outside-service stack: HT → Anodize → FAI inspection — 12–18 business day lead time

Three external hand-offs (heat treat per AMS 2770, hard-coat anodize per MIL-A-8625 Type III, source-inspected FAI). Each adds vendor minimums and shipping time. Cumulative lead time is the dominant cost driver on this part — verify with each vendor before committing to delivery.

Recommended: Get firm lead-time + min-lot quotes from HT and anodize vendors before pricing.

Location: page 1 title block (HT spec), page 3 (anodize spec), PO (FAI)

PURPLE · CLARIFICATION · CONFIDENCE 60%
Title-block UOS tolerance (±0.005") may conflict with implied ±0.001" on press-fit pin location

Pin-location callout on view B-B reads "TYP" without explicit tolerance. UOS title-block tolerance is ±0.005", but a press-fit pin typically requires ±0.001" or tighter. Cannot confirm intent from the document alone.

Recommended: Email customer: "Please confirm pin-location tolerance — UOS or tighter?"

Location: view B-B, pin callout
