🛑 Must resolve before quoting
Blockers, and the exact questions to send the customer.

COMPLIANCE· CONFIDENCE 95% Capability check · vendor
NADCAP heat-treat certification required for AS9100 deliverable
Action
Confirm a NADCAP-certified heat-treat vendor is available before quoting lead time.
Drawing references AMS 2770 (heat treatment of wrought aluminum). Combined with the AS9100 quality clause on page 4, the heat-treat processor must hold a current NADCAP HT certification. Verify the shop-approved vendor list before committing.
📍 page 1 title block + page 4, quality clause QC-7
SEQUENCE· CONFIDENCE 90% Sequence · scrap risk
Hard-coat anodize specified after final machining — masking required for ±0.0005" bores
Action
Clarify with the customer: mask the bores during anodize, or pre-machine oversize and post-anodize hone to size?
Two precision bores (Ø 0.5000 +0.0005/-0.0000) are dimensioned post-anodize per note 7. Hard Coat Type III adds 0.0005–0.001" per side. The bores must be masked during anodize OR machined oversize pre-anodize — the print does not specify which.
📍 page 2, note 7 + bores at view A-A
📋 Questions to send the customer
  1. Can you confirm a NADCAP-certified heat-treat vendor is acceptable for the AMS 2770 heat treatment?BLOCKING
    The AS9100 clause requires a NADCAP-certified processor; vendor availability sets the lead time.
  2. For the Ø0.5000 +0.0005/-0.0000 bores: should they be masked during anodize, or pre-machined oversize and honed to size afterward?BLOCKING
    Determines pre-anodize bore dimensions and whether masking labor is in the routing.
  3. Is a source-inspected AS9102 First Article required before the production run, and on what timeline?IMPORTANT
    FAI documentation is billable NRE and adds 5–10 days before production can ramp.
  4. Please confirm the pin-location tolerance on view B-B — is it the UOS ±0.005", or tighter for the press fit?NICE TO HAVE
    Press fits usually need ±0.001" or tighter; affects inspection scope.

🏭 Outside Services · 1
OUTSIDE_SERVICE· CONFIDENCE 82% Lead-time · dominant cost
Outside-service stack: HT → Anodize → FAI — 12–18 business day lead time
Action
Get firm lead-time and minimum-lot quotes from the HT and anodize vendors before pricing.
Three external hand-offs (heat treat per AMS 2770, hard-coat anodize per MIL-A-8625 Type III, source-inspected FAI). Each adds vendor minimums and shipping time; cumulative lead time is the dominant cost driver on this part.

First Article Inspection per AS9102 required before production
Action

🔎 Secondary checks & verify (2) — low-confidence AI checks and nice-to-have questions
CLARIFICATION· CONFIDENCE 60%
Title-block UOS tolerance (±0.005") may conflict with implied ±0.001" on press-fit pin location
Action
Email the customer: "Please confirm the pin-location tolerance — UOS or tighter?"
The pin-location callout on view B-B reads "TYP" without an explicit tolerance. The UOS title-block tolerance is ±0.005", but a press-fit pin typically requires ±0.001" or tighter. Intent cannot be confirmed from the document alone.
